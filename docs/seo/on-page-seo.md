## Rules applied site-wide

- Exactly one H1 per page (enforced by the audit; the admin rich-text sanitiser downgrades pasted H1s to H2).
- Self-referencing absolute canonical on every indexable page; paginated lists canonicalise to their own `?page=N`.
- `noindex,follow` on `/thank-you/`, `/landing/*`, empty listing pages, and all `/admin/*`.
- OG/Twitter tags on every page; default share image `/assets/img/og-default.jpg`, replaced by the
  page image where one exists.
- Primary keyword appears in the title, H1 and first paragraph — written naturally, never repeated to hit a count.
- Every content image has descriptive alt text (admin enforces a prompt; the audit fails on a missing `alt`).
- Each page has a visible phone CTA and (except legal pages) a WhatsApp CTA.

## Empty-state handling

`/projects/`, `/reviews/` and `/blog/` become `index,follow` and enter the sitemap only once they have
content. Until then they are `noindex,follow` so Google never sees an empty page.
