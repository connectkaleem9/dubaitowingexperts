## Patterns to learn from

1. **Phone + WhatsApp everywhere**; WhatsApp prefilled messages are standard. We match this.
2. **No published prices** — industry norm is quote-on-contact. Our "Get a quote" flow fits.
3. **Dubai-specific services** that searchers use: *basement pull-out* (tower car parks in
   Marina/Business Bay/JLT/Downtown have low clearance), *desert/sand pull-out*, *flatbed*
   for luxury/low cars, *motorbike recovery*, *EV recovery*, highway (Sheikh Zayed Rd, E311,
   E611) breakdowns.
4. **Area pages at scale** (33–78). Many are near-duplicates — a weakness and an SEO risk
   (doorway pages). We will ship fewer, genuinely distinct area pages.
5. **Long in-depth guide on homepage** is common; we will put guides in `/blog/` and link
   them, keeping the homepage fast and conversion-focused.
6. **Trust signals** rely on ratings, licences, stats. We **cannot** claim any of these
   until the owner confirms them (CLAUDE.md §2). Our trust must come from real reviews,
   real project photos, clear process, transparent quoting, and a real phone number.

## Gaps / opportunities for Dubai Towing Experts

- **Genuine project gallery with details** (service, area, vehicle type) — most competitors
  show generic stock or unlabelled photos. Our Projects system is a differentiator.
- **Moderated first-party reviews** with service tags.
- **Helpful accident guidance** (police report via Dubai Police app / DubaiNow, what to do
  before the truck arrives) — competitors bury this; it serves informational intent and
  builds trust.
- **Distinct area pages** built around real road/parking context rather than name swaps.
- **Fast mobile experience**: many competitor pages are heavy builder sites.

## Claims competitors make that we must NOT copy without owner evidence

24/7 availability, "30-minute arrival", "RTA licensed", ratings/review counts, years of
experience, fleet size, insurer partnerships, "cheapest", "no hidden fees" guarantees.
